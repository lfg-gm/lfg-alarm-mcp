# LFG Alarm

LFG Alarm rings real alarms on the person's phone (iPhone and Android) and laptop. Its tools set alarms for a time, a token's price, a post or a live stream, a trader's or a wallet's trades, an event link, or a call to a private web address, and dress them with a sound, a wallpaper and a note.

The first time a tool runs, sign in with `/mcp auth lfg-alarm`: a browser opens on lfg.gm, where the person signs in with their LFG Alarm account and taps Allow.
